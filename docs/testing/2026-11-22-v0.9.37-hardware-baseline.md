The first 60-second monitor attempt saw only reconnect retries. The Cardputer later re-enumerated and produced a second 60-second telemetry capture in `logs/serial-60s-20261122-133455.log`.

The 60-second capture followed the verified upload and contained the diagnostic-only `[MEM-BASE]` / `[BLOCK]` markers, but it began after boot (device uptime was about 164 s); the `[BUILD]` identity banner was not captured. A later 180-second capture (`logs/serial-180s-20261122-133930.log`) recorded one reset (`Reset Reason: 1`) and a complete startup through `setup() complete`; the serial monitor reconnected after the early boot banner, so `[BUILD]` was again missed. The binary itself contains source commit `8bfc5b8143fe816d2b42972d625206f277dc4a45`, verified with `strings`. The exact BIN SHA and successful esptool write are recorded above; runtime logs are associated with that upload by sequence, MAC, and diagnostic markers, though the device does not attest the BIN SHA over serial.

Observed idle window (no musical workflow was performed):
