- baseline ELF contains `Wavetable::triangleTable_`;
- WT1 ELF does not contain `Wavetable::triangleTable_`.

The production caller census independently proved that `lookupTriangle` had no production caller outside its own inline accessor and that the triangle representation was confined to `src/dsp/audio_wavetables.h/.cpp`.

The WT1 no-resurrection guard also requires `sawTable_` and `squareTable_` to survive, keeping the checkpoint scoped to triangle-only removal.
